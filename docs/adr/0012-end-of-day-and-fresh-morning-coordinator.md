
**Amendment (2026-11-11): the morning day rule.** A fresh coordinator starts on the first open of a day only if the newest journal file is at least as new as the coordinator's last use (its newest conversation entry that is not a Dovetail notice; a file written within 10 minutes before that still counts, since the handoff's tail, a hand-written file's "done" and a `:fresh` seed's first reply land just after it). Otherwise the existing coordinator is kept (resumed as on any other open) and Needs you gets an item, "<Day> wasn't closed; kept that coordinator. `:eod` to close it.", where <Day> is the weekday of the last use (with the date when it is a week or more back). This stops a day being lost when the newest journal is older than the last use; it also means a `:fresh` followed by more work, with no `:eod`, is not seeded from the stale mid-day handoff. The "Keep yesterday's coordinator" item is unchanged.
